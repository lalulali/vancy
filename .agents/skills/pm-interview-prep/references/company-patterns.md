# Company Patterns (heuristics, verify with the actual loop)

Treat as hypotheses from commonly shared candidate reports; confirm with the recruiter, who often describes the loop. Never claim these are official.

| Company type | Typical emphasis | Adapt |
|---|---|---|
| Big tech consumer | Product sense, metrics, execution; strong structured frameworks; technical for some | Practise product design and metric root-cause; speak in clear structure |
| Big tech enterprise/cloud | Strategy, technical depth, customer empathy, GTM | Study B2B pricing, buyer vs. user, partner ecosystems |
| Startups / scale-ups | Execution, ownership, speed, ambiguity, take-home | Show prioritisation under constraint and scrappy validation |
| Fintech / marketplaces | Metrics, risk/fraud, two-sided dynamics, regulation | Liquidity, take rate, unit economics, trust and safety |
| AI labs / AI-first | AI product sense, evals, safety, technical depth, strategy | Use `ai-pm.md` thoroughly; know model limits, cost, safety trade-offs |
| Agencies / consultancies | Case-style strategy and estimation | Practise market sizing and structured MECE thinking |
| Non-tech incumbents (bank, retail) | Strategy, stakeholder/exec management, digital transformation | Change management, regulation, legacy constraints |

## Adapting by role
- **Technical PM:** weight technical acumen higher; expect system design and API discussion.
- **Growth PM:** metrics, experiments, funnels, pricing.
- **Platform/API PM:** developers as users, adoption, versioning, SLAs.
- **AI PM:** evals, model/data lifecycle, safety, cost, human-in-loop.
- **Senior/Group:** strategy, vision, org trade-offs, portfolio.

## Preparing for a specific company
1. Use their product daily for a week; note 5 frustrations and 5 delights.
2. Read their mission, latest earnings or launch posts, and 2 competitors (`[verify]` stats).
3. Prepare 3 improvement ideas, 2 monetisation ideas, 1 metric set, 1 strategic risk.
4. Prepare questions for the interviewer (product direction, success measures, team collaboration).
5. Run a mock in their likely style.
