# Cover Letter Review Rubric

Score each dimension 0–5, then multiply by its weight. The maximum total is 100. Every score must be backed by quoted evidence. Show the sum line in the report (for example `3×4 + 2×4 + … = 62`); the headline score must equal it. Source numbers refer to `evidence.md`.

| # | Dimension | Weight | 5 = | 0–1 = |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specificity and tailoring** | 4 | Names the role and company. At least one verifiable company-specific fact. Addresses the job ad's top requirements in the ad's own terms. Passes the any-company test [13][16][50] | Generic. Could be sent anywhere. Company named only in the salutation |
| 2 | **Employer focus and value** | 4 | Frames the letter around the employer's needs. Names a concrete contribution ("first 90 days…"). "You/your" is present. `i_to_you_ratio` is a rough guide, not a pass mark | All about the candidate ("I am… I have… I want…"). Asks what the job gives the candidate |
| 3 | **Proof and metrics** | 3 | 1–3 relevant, quantified achievements. The strongest one ends the proof paragraph. All match the CV | Claims without evidence. No numbers. Numbers contradict the CV |
| 4 | **Adds beyond the CV** | 2 | Gives context, motivation, a bridge for a career change, or an explanation of a gap. Selects; does not list | Restates CV bullets in prose |
| 5 | **Opening and close** | 2 | P1 hooks within 2 lines with a specific reason and proof. A courteous, confident call to action | "I am writing to apply…". Weak or no call to action. Desperate or presumptuous tone |
| 6 | **Structure and length** | 2 | Body is 250–400 words in 3–5 paragraphs on one page, or within the form's limit. Logical flow [13][30] | Over one page or under 150 words. Wall of text. Bullets only |
| 7 | **Tone and language** | 2 | Warm and professional. No clichés or typos. One spelling convention. Register suits the company | Clichés, stock enthusiasm, typos, gimmicks, overly formal |
| 8 | **Conventions and accuracy** | 1 | Salutation and sign-off pair correctly for the market. Recipient name spelled correctly. Contact details identical to the CV. No placeholders or personal data | "To whom it may concern". "Faithfully" with a named recipient. Mismatched details. "[Company]" left in |

Bands: **85+** ready · **70–84** targeted fixes · **50–69** significant rewrite · **<50** rewrite from scratch (offer cover-letter-builder).

## Severity
- **Critical**: wrong company or role name, a leftover placeholder, a factual mismatch with the CV, a misspelled recipient name, an offensive or risky joke.
- **High**: generic content, no proof, focused on the candidate instead of the employer, more than one page.
- **Medium**: weak opener or close, clichés, a CV recap, mixed spelling.
- **Low**: polish.
