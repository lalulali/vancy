# Scripts and Emails

Adapt every template to real facts. Remove square brackets before sending. Never include fabricated figures, offers, or claims.

## 1. Asking for the range (early in the process)
> "Thank you for asking. To make sure we are aligned, could you share the budgeted range for this role? Once I understand the scope, I can tell you whether it fits."

If pressed, give a researched range anchored to your evidence:
> "Based on the scope we discussed and what I see for comparable roles, I'm looking at [low] to [high] total, depending on the full package."

## 2. Receiving the offer (phone)
> "Thank you, I'm excited about the role and about working with [team/person]. Could you send the details in writing, including salary, bonus, benefits and start date? I'd like to review it properly and come back to you by [date]."

## 3. Asking for time
> "I'm very interested in joining [Company]. To give you a considered answer, could I take until [date]? Is that workable on your side?"

## 4. Counteroffer email (salary)
```
Subject: [Role] offer - thank you

Hi [Name],

Thank you again for the offer. I'm excited about [specific thing about the role/team] and I'd like to join [Company].

After reviewing the full package, I'd like to discuss the base salary. Based on [market data for comparable roles / the scope of the role, including X / my experience delivering Y], I was hoping for a base of [number]. [OPTIONAL, only if you will truly accept: If we can agree on that figure and the other terms in the written offer, I would be glad to accept.]

I'm also glad to talk through the other parts of the package if that is more flexible. Could we find a time to speak this week?

Thank you,
[Name]
```
Rules: one request; one reason; enthusiasm; an open door. **Say this only if you will accept** at that figure: the accept line is conditional on the number and on the written terms, but it can still be read as a commitment, and a bluff damages trust. If you are not sure, leave it out and write "I would like to find a way to make this work." instead.

## 5. Counteroffer email (non-salary)
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the offer; I'm keen to accept. I understand the base salary sits within a fixed band. Could we discuss a few other items?

- A sign-on bonus of [amount] to bridge [reason, e.g. a bonus I forfeit by leaving]
- A pay and level review at six months, with agreed goals
- [One additional day of leave / two remote days / a learning budget of X]

If we can agree on these, I'm happy to confirm my acceptance. Do you have time for a quick call this week?

Best,
[Name]
```
Use only items that the user actually values, and only claim a forfeited bonus if it is true.

## 6. Responding to "that is our best offer"
> "I understand, and I appreciate you checking. Given that, is there flexibility on [sign-on / review date / leave / remote days]? And what would I need to show in the first six months to move toward [target]?"

## 7. Accepting
```
Subject: Acceptance - [Role]

Hi [Name],

I'm delighted to accept the offer for [Role] at [Company], with a start date of [date], [base] base, [other agreed terms]. Thank you for working with me on this.

Please send the final contract and let me know what you need from me before I start.

Best,
[Name]
```
Confirm that all agreed terms appear in the written contract **before** resigning from your current job.

## 8. Declining
```
Subject: [Role] offer

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the offer and for the time you and the team spent with me. After careful consideration, I've decided not to proceed, because [short, honest reason such as "I've accepted a role that is a closer match for my current goals"].

I really enjoyed learning about [specific thing], and I hope our paths cross again.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

## 9. Asking for a raise or promotion review
> "I'd like to discuss my role and compensation. Over the last [period] I've [two or three results with numbers], and my scope has grown to include [responsibility]. Based on [market data / the role's level], I'd like to explore moving to [title/range]. Could we schedule time to talk this week?"
Bring a one-page summary of results and the evidence. Ask what the process and timing are.
