# Worked Review Example (CV)

A complete, realistic application of the rubric. Use it as a model for depth, tone, and the before/after format. The candidate is fictional; target role: **Senior Product Manager, B2B SaaS**. All names and numbers in this example are fictional; never reuse them or any figure the user didn't give. Any resemblance to real people or companies is coincidental.

## Input (excerpt of the CV under review)
```
JON SMITH
Product Manager | jon@email

MY JOURNEY
Dedicated, results-driven product professional and team player with a proven
track record and excellent communication skills.

EXPERIENCE
Product Manager, Tillbridge (2022-present)
- Responsible for the product roadmap
- Worked on checkout and helped improve conversion
- Involved in many cross-functional meetings
- Successfully managed stakeholders

Product Manager, Corvane (2018-2021)
- Duties included owning the invoicing product
- Helped grow the API

EDUCATION
BSc Computer Science
References available upon request
```

## Review output

# CV Review: Jon Smith, target: Senior Product Manager (B2B SaaS, UK)

**Score: 30/100 (rebuild)**: the experience looks relevant, but nothing is quantified, the headings are non-standard, and the summary uses stock phrases that any candidate could write. This needs a rebuild, not a polish.

| Dimension | Weight | Score | Key evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Targeting & keyword fit | 4 | 1/5 | Headline "Product Manager" has no level or domain; ad terms (experimentation, pricing, stakeholder management) absent |
| First-skim clarity | 3 | 1/5 | No metrics; "MY JOURNEY" buries the pitch; scope of each role unknown |
| Achievement focus & quantification | 4 | 1/5 | 0% of bullets quantified; all duties ("Responsible for", "Helped", "Involved in") |
| Language quality | 2 | 1/5 | Stock phrases ("results-driven", "team player", "proven track record"); weak openers throughout |
| Structure & length | 2 | 2/5 | Non-standard headings; no Key Skills section; ~0.5 page (thin for senior) |
| ATS & accessibility | 3 | 2/5 | Email only, no phone; "MY JOURNEY" heading not mapped; otherwise text is clean |
| Consistency & credibility | 1 | 3/5 | Dates plausible but year-only; name and email agree |
| Compliance & bias safety | 1 | 4/5 | No risky personal data; drop "References available upon request" |

Weighted sum: 1×4 + 1×3 + 1×4 + 1×2 + 2×2 + 2×3 + 3×1 + 4×1 = 4+3+4+2+4+6+3+4 = **30/100** (band: below 50 = rebuild).

## Top fixes (ranked by impact)
1. **High: Quantify every achievement.** Recruiters read titles, employers and dates first and give the top third of page one most attention,[^4][^24] so scale and results should be visible there, and none are. Stanford GSB tells candidates to be as quantitative as possible.[^37]
   - Before: "Worked on checkout and helped improve conversion"
   - After: "Increased checkout conversion from [metric?: baseline]% to [metric?: result]% by [fact?: what you changed], adding [metric?: revenue impact]"
2. **High: Add a target-matched headline and summary.** A generic headline gives a recruiter's search nothing to match,[^2] and the top third of page one carries the most weight.[^24]
   - After (headline): "Product Manager | B2B SaaS | Payments" [fact?: confirm your domains]
   - After (summary L1): "Product manager with [N] years in B2B SaaS and payments [confirm: roles span 2018–present], working on checkout used by [metric?: merchant count] merchants [fact?: confirm your ownership level]."
3. **High: Replace duty bullets with scope + achievements.** Lead each role with a scope bullet (team, budget, users, reporting line), then a Key Achievements block using the XYZ formula.[^33]
4. **High: Standard headings + Key Skills section.** Rename "MY JOURNEY" → "Professional Summary"; add "Key Skills" near the top with the ad's true terms (experimentation, pricing, stakeholder management). Unclear or inconsistent sections cause parse problems.[^27]
5. **Medium: Remove stock phrases and weak openers.** Cut "results-driven / team player / proven track record"; start bullets with verbs (Led, Grew, Launched). Generic, non-personalised content is a common rejection reason.[^8]
6. **Medium: Add a phone number; drop "References available upon request".** Keep contact details in the body.[^27] The phrase uses space better spent on content; the UK National Careers Service accepts it, but US sources call it outdated.[^74][^31]

## What's working (keep)
- Two relevant B2B product roles with clear progression (Corvane 2018–2021, then Tillbridge from 2022); owning the invoicing product and growing an API are real scope signals once quantified.
- Clean, single-column plain text with no tables or images to fix, so nothing is lost when the file is parsed.

## Keyword coverage (vs the ad)
Present: product roadmap, cross-functional. Missing but likely true (ask): experimentation, pricing, stakeholder management, A/B testing. Missing, confirm: payments domain. Coverage is information for the conversation, not a pass mark.

## Questions for you
- What were the real conversion, revenue, team-size, and merchant-count numbers?
- Which of the missing ad terms are genuinely true of your experience?
