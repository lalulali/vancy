# Message Templates

Adapt to the real people and facts. Remove square brackets before sending. Never send a request to someone on the user's behalf; draft it for the user to send.

## 1. Asking someone to be a referee
```
Subject: Would you be willing to be a reference?

Hi [Name],

I hope you are well. I am applying for [type of role] roles, and I would be grateful if you would be willing to act as a reference for me. We worked together on [project/team] from [year] to [year], and I valued [specific thing].

I will make sure you have my CV and the role details so that it is easy. Please tell me honestly if you would rather not, or if the timing does not suit you.

One more thing: I use an AI assistant to help prepare my application documents, and your name and contact details would be processed by it. If you are not comfortable with that, you are welcome to decline, and I will understand.

Thank you for considering it.

Best regards,
[Name]
```
Rules: give them an easy way to decline; do not assume yes. Keep the AI-assistant sentence (or adapt it to the tool the user really uses) so the referee can make an informed choice. Delete it only if no AI tool will see their details.

## 2. Briefing a referee (after they agree)
```
Subject: Reference details for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for agreeing to be a reference. Here are the details:

- Role: [title] at [company], [short description or link]
- Why it fits: they are looking for [two requirements from the job brief]
- Points that would help if they come up: [project A and its result], [skill B], [quality C]
- My CV is attached for reference.
- The best contact details to use: [email, phone, time zone]. Please tell me if you prefer something else.

I will let you know when I expect them to get in touch.

Thank you again,
[Name]
```

## 3. Notifying a referee that a check is coming
> "Hi [Name], [Company] has asked to speak to my references, and I have given them your details. They may contact you in the next [week]. Thank you again for your help. Please tell me if you want me to resend my CV or the role details."

## 4. Thanking a referee
> "Hi [Name], thank you for speaking with [Company]. [I got the role / I am still in the process / I did not get the role this time], and your support made a real difference. I appreciate it very much, and I would be glad to return the favour any time."

## 5. Asking for a written reference letter
```
Subject: Request for a reference letter

Hi [Name],

I am preparing applications for [type of role] and I would be grateful for a short reference letter if you are willing to write one. It would help if it covered [two or three points: your role together, a project and its result, a strength]. I have attached my CV and a summary of the role, and I am happy to provide a draft of bullet points to make it easier.

If you agree, it would help to have it by [date]. Please tell me if that is not possible.

Thank you,
[Name]
```
Rules: it is acceptable to offer bullet points, but the referee must say it in their own words and approve it. Never write a reference for yourself and sign it as someone else.

## 6. Replying to an employer who asks for references
```
Subject: References - [Name], [Role]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the update. I am glad to provide references. I have attached a one-page sheet with [number] professional referees, who have each agreed to be contacted. Their preferred contact methods and time zones are listed.

Could you let me know when you plan to contact them, so I can let them know to expect it?

Best regards,
[Name]
```
If the employer asks to wait until an offer, or the user's current employer should not be contacted yet, say so politely and early.
