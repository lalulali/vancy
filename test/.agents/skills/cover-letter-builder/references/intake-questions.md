# Intake Questions (Cover Letter)

Ask only for what is missing. Group the questions 3–5 per message.

## Essentials
1. Job ad (paste it, or give the link), job title, and reference number if any.
2. Company name, and the team or department.
3. Hiring manager's name and title, if known. (Ask whether they can find it on the job ad, LinkedIn or the company site, or by calling.)
4. Candidate's CV, or the cv-builder output. If there's no CV: current role, 2–3 best achievements with metrics, years of experience, specialisms.
5. Target market/country, and how the application will be sent: attached letter, email body, or portal field (with its character or word limit).

## What makes it specific
6. Why this company? A product you use, a strategy, news, values, a mission, people you know there.
7. A referral or contact? (Name it only with their permission.)
8. Which job-ad requirement do you match best? Which one do you worry about?
9. A first contribution you could make (an idea, a fix, a 30/60/90-day focus)?

## Situations to address (only if relevant)
10. Career change: what is the bridge between the old field and the new one?
11. Gap or return to work: a brief, honest reason, plus how you have kept current.
12. Relocation or work authorisation: are you moving, and do you need sponsorship?
13. Salary, notice period or availability: include these only if the job ad asks for them.

## Preferences
14. Tone: formal, warm professional (the default), or conversational (startups and creative roles).
15. Template: the same as the CV (recommended). Name the CV's template if it is known.
