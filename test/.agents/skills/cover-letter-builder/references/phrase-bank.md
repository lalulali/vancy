# Phrase Bank (adapt, never paste verbatim)

> All names and numbers here are fictional; never reuse them or any figure the user did not give.

These are starting shapes. Every phrase must be filled in with specific facts. If a sentence could go into any company's letter, rewrite it.

## Openers (P1)
1. When I read that [Company] is [initiative/news], I knew the [Role] opening was one I had to pursue.
2. [Company]'s work on [product/goal] is the problem I have spent [N] years solving at [Employer].
3. Over [N] years in [field], I have [headline result]. I would like to do the same as your [Role].
4. I am not actively looking to move, but the [Role] role at [Company] matches my experience too closely to pass up. (Use only if true (passive candidate).)
5. Having worked on both [side A] and [side B] of [domain], I understand what [Company]'s [customers/partners] need.
6. [Referrer], [their role] on your [team], suggested I contact you about the [Role] position.
7. As a [degree] graduate who has already [achievement], I am keen to bring that experience to [Company]'s [team].
8. After [N] years in [previous field], I am moving into [new field], bringing [transferable strength] that [Company] needs for [goal].
9. I use [Company product] every [day/week], and I see exactly where a [Role] could take it next.
10. [Company]'s goal of [goal from JD or research] is one I have already delivered once: [metric].

## Transitions (P2–P3)
1. In my current role as [Title] at [Employer], I …
2. The result I am proudest of is …
3. Before that, at [Employer], I …
4. That experience taught me to …
5. Building on this, …
6. What connects these roles is …
7. Outside my formal work, I … (graduates, volunteering)
8. After a planned career break for [reason], I [refreshed skill/credential] …
9. Equally relevant to this role is …
10. The same approach applies directly to [JD requirement].

## Value statements (P4)
1. Your focus on [priority] matches my record of [achievement].
2. I can help [Company] [goal] by applying the [method] that delivered [result] at [Employer].
3. My combination of [skill A] and [skill B] is unusual in [field] and directly relevant to [team need].
4. In my first [90 days], I would focus on [specific contribution].
5. Because I have worked on the [stakeholder] side, I understand [their concern] first-hand.
6. I am used to working across [markets/time zones/cultures], which suits [Company]'s [distributed/international] team.
7. I have studied [product/market] and have early ideas on [opportunity] that I would be glad to discuss.
8. Leading teams of [N] has prepared me to help [Company] scale [function].
9. I would bring a tested approach to [challenge named in the JD].
10. Where my figures are confidential, I would be happy to share more context at interview.

## Calls to action (P5)
1. I would welcome a conversation about how I could contribute to [team/goal].
2. I would be glad to walk you through [project] and how it applies to [Company]'s plans.
3. Could we arrange a short call in the coming weeks?
4. I am available for an interview at your convenience.
5. I would value the chance to share my ideas on [opportunity].
6. Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.
7. My CV is attached for your review.
8. Please let me know if any further information would help.

## Sign-offs (match the market; see regional-conventions.md)
- "Sincerely," (US and neutral default)
- "Yours sincerely," (UK/Commonwealth, named recipient; the traditional form)
- "Yours faithfully," (UK/Commonwealth, only with "Dear Sir or Madam"; never with a name)
- "Kind regards," (UK/EU/AU; accepted in modern business writing and a common international default)
- "Best regards," (international email)
- "Respectfully," (formal, government)

## Avoid (lint flags most of these)
"To whom it may concern" · "I am writing to apply/express my interest" (as the opener) · "It is with great enthusiasm…" · "Please find attached my CV" (as the opener) · "perfect fit" / "ideal candidate" (unbacked) · "team player", "hard-working", "passionate", "dynamic" · "rapidly changing world" · "This role would be a great opportunity for me" (frames the letter around what you get, not what they get) · repeating the same sentence twice.
