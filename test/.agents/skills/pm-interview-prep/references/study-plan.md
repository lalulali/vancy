# Study Plans

Start with the diagnostic; weight time toward the lowest-scoring categories. Each session: 1 learn block, 1 mock, 1 score and re-do.

## 1-week sprint (about 2 h/day)
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnostic (3 categories); read frameworks; set priorities |
| 2 | Product Sense: learn + 2 mocks |
| 3 | Metrics: define metrics + metric drop + A/B test |
| 4 | Strategy or Execution (by role); 2 mocks |
| 5 | Technical + Estimation drills (AI: `ai-pm.md`) |
| 6 | Full mixed mock (3 questions), strict timing |
| 7 | Review weak areas, company research, rest |

## 2-week plan
Week 1: one category per day (Product Sense, Metrics, Strategy, Execution, Technical, Estimation), each = learn + mock + re-do; day 7 mixed mock.
Week 2: weakest 3 categories twice; AI-PM layer (if AI track); one panel and one take-home simulation; company-specific prep; final mixed mock 2 days before.

## 4-week plan (career switchers / new to PM)
Week 1: PM fundamentals (product lifecycle, discovery, roadmaps, user research), 3 products teardown. Week 2: Product Sense + Metrics deep dive. Week 3: Strategy, Execution, Technical, Estimation. Week 4: mixed mocks (2/week), AI layer, company prep, tapering.

## Daily micro-habits (15-20 min)
- 1 estimation problem; 1 product teardown (user, pain, 1 improvement); 1 "metric dropped" checklist run; 1 technical concept explained in 60 s (record yourself).

## Weekly review
Score trend by category, top recurring gap, one habit to change, next week's focus. Keep the log (`PM_Interview_Log.jsonl`).

## Drills library
- **Clarify-only:** 5 prompts, 3 clarifying questions each.
- **Persona sprint:** 3 segments in 2 min, choose one.
- **Idea storm:** 10 ideas in 3 min, cluster, pick one.
- **RICE aloud:** rank 6 features in 4 min.
- **Metric tree:** North Star -> inputs -> guardrail in 5 min.
- **Fermi 5x5:** 5 problems, 5 min each, cross-check each.
- **Explain it:** 5 tech concepts to a 10-year-old.
- **90-second answer:** compress any answer to 90 s, then expand.
- **AI eval drill:** for 5 AI features, write the golden set, metric and guardrail.

## Day before / day of
Sleep; review cheat cards (not new material); prepare questions to ask; test tech; pen and paper; drink water. During: breathe, clarify, structure, recommend.

## Resources to suggest (verify availability)
Books: *Cracking the PM Interview*, *Decode and Conquer*, *Inspired*, *Escaping the Build Trap*, *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*; for AI: provider docs on evals and responsible AI, and writing about LLM product design. Practise with peers when possible.
