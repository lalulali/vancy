# Adapting by Career Stage and Industry

## Career stage
| Stage | Length | Emphasis | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student / new graduate | 1 page (US) / 1–2 | Education first (degree, expected date, GPA if strong, modules, thesis); internships written like jobs (scope + achievements); projects; leadership in clubs/volunteering with numbers (funds raised, members, events) | Don't pad with school-level detail; quantify activities |
| Early career (<5 yrs) | 1–2 | Fast learning and early ownership; promotions; concrete outputs | Avoid duty lists; 1 role may carry most weight |
| Mid (5–10 yrs) | 2 | Specialism + measurable impact; leading small teams/projects | Trim early roles to 1–2 bullets |
| Senior / manager (10+) | 2 (a 3rd page is an unsourced convention in some markets; UK and Germany expect ≤ 2) | Scope (team, budget, P&L, geography), strategy, transformation, stakeholders | Compress roles >15 yrs old; avoid adjective-heavy summaries |
| Executive (Dir/VP/C-suite) | 2 (3 only as an unsourced executive convention; UK and Germany expect ≤ 2) | P&L, growth, M&A/IPO, turnarounds, board exposure, org building; 3 signature wins in summary | Every line must be strategic; use `executive` template |
| Career changer | 2 | Hybrid format; summary bridges old → new ("Former X turned Y"); transferable achievements reframed in target vocabulary; relevant courses/projects | Don't hide past field — translate it |
| Returner / gap | 1–2 | One honest line on the gap; recent upskilling, volunteering, freelance | Don't fake dates |
| Academic | No limit | Publications, grants, teaching, supervision, service, talks | Use academic CV conventions, not a 2-page resume |

## Industry patterns
| Industry | Put up front | Typical metrics | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software / IT / data | Grouped technical skills (languages, frameworks, cloud, data), GitHub/portfolio | Users, latency, uptime, cost savings, deploy frequency, model accuracy/lift, revenue impact | Name the method/model and the outcome; "Selected Projects" blocks (project, stack, role, result) |
| Product / UX | Product areas, discovery, experimentation & GTM | Conversion, retention, activation, NPS, revenue/ARR, adoption | Show product decisions and trade-offs, not just shipping features |
| AI Product Management | AI/ML product surface, evaluation rigour, model/system trade-offs | Task completion/acceptance rate, offline/online eval precision/recall, hallucination/fallback rate, p95 latency, cost per 1k tokens or query, revenue lift | Show why AI beat a deterministic baseline, how quality was evaluated, and how safety/cost were governed |
| Finance / accounting / audit | Credentials (CPA/ACCA/CFA), standards (IFRS, US GAAP, SOX) | Close time, audit findings, savings, AUM, deal value, forecast accuracy | Confidential numbers → % or ranges |
| Banking / risk / compliance | Regulations and frameworks (generic: AML/KYC, Basel, credit risk) | Portfolio size, losses/NPL, findings closed, remediation time | Avoid country-specific regulator jargon unless target market uses it |
| Sales / business development | Quota attainment, segments, deal size | % of target, revenue, new logos, win rate, ranking, streaks, club awards | Lead every role with attainment |
| Marketing / digital | Channels, tools, budgets | CAC, ROAS, CTR, conversion, pipeline, share of voice, followers→revenue | Tie vanity metrics to business results |
| Engineering (mech/civil/elec) | Specialism, registration (PE/CEng), standards, tools (CAD/FEA) | Project value, schedule, safety record, defects, cost | Project list with client/value/role |
| Operations / supply chain | Network scope, sites, volume | OTIF, inventory, cost per unit, cycle time, safety | Before→after pairs work well |
| HR / people | Headcount supported, programmes | Time-to-hire, retention/turnover, engagement, cost per hire | |
| Admin / office / EA | Who you support (exec level, team size), systems | Hours saved, cost cuts, events run, process digitised | Achievements can be efficiency and reliability |
| Healthcare | Licences, specialties, settings, patient volume | Outcomes, throughput, safety, compliance | Licences prominent; protect patient data |
| Education | Subjects, levels, class sizes, curriculum | Pass rates, attainment gains, programme adoption | |
| Hospitality / retail / service | Property/brand tier, team size | Guest ratings, upsell %, complaints, sales per head | |
| Creative / writing / design | Portfolio link (required), clients, publications | Reach, circulation, awards, campaign results | Design flair belongs in the portfolio; keep the CV ATS-safe |
| Public sector / NGO | Programmes, stakeholders, funding | Beneficiaries, budget, policy outcomes | Some governments use mandated formats — follow the posting |
