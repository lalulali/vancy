Senior Product Manager, Merchant Onboarding
Northwind Payments - Hybrid (London, 3 days in office)
Ref EXAMPLE-001

About us
Northwind Payments helps small businesses get paid faster. We are a fast-paced, ambitious team of 450 across 8 European markets.

The role
You will own the end-to-end merchant onboarding experience for our new instant payouts product. Our activation rate has stalled and onboarding takes too long, so this is a role for someone who can fix the funnel and work with risk and compliance.

What you'll do
- Own the roadmap for merchant onboarding and identity verification
- Run weekly experiments and report conversion and activation results to the leadership team
- Partner with risk, compliance and engineering to reduce fraud without hurting conversion
- Lead a squad of 8 engineers, 2 designers and 1 data analyst
- Work with pricing and finance on packaging for instant payouts

What you'll need
- 6+ years of product management experience in B2B SaaS or payments
- Proven experience running A/B tests and experimentation programmes
- Strong stakeholder management skills across risk, legal and engineering
- Experience with KYC / AML flows or identity verification
- Strong SQL skills
- Must have the right to work in the UK

Nice to have
- Experience with PSD2 and 3DS
- Exposure to usage-based pricing
- Familiarity with Looker or Amplitude

What we offer
Competitive salary, 25 days holiday, pension, private health cover, learning budget.
