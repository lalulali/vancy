# Copy Patterns for LinkedIn

All names and numbers here are fictional; never reuse them or any figure the user did not give.

Adapt every pattern to real facts. Never paste a pattern with placeholders still in it.

## 1. Headline formulas (<= 220 chars)
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Role \| Niche \| Tools/Proof | `Senior Product Manager \| B2B payments & onboarding \| Experimentation, pricing, identity verification` |
| Role who [outcome] | `Data analyst who turns messy sales data into weekly decisions \| SQL, Tableau, Python` |
| Role \| Credential \| Domain | `Chartered Accountant (ACCA) \| Group reporting & audit readiness \| IFRS` |
| Career changer bridge | `Former teacher turned UX researcher \| Mixed-methods research \| Education & edtech` |
| Student / graduate | `Computer Science graduate \| Backend Python & cloud \| Graduate software roles` (use sparingly) |
| Executive | `COO \| Scaled operations from 3 to 11 countries \| Supply chain, P&L, M&A integration` |
Rules: use searchable job-title words, specialise, show proof; skip "passionate", "guru", "ninja".

## 2. About structures
**A. Proof-first (default)**
1. What you do + scale + one number.
2. Signature results (2-3 short sentences).
3. How you work or what you bring (3 bullets max).
4. What you want next + how to reach you.

**B. Narrative bridge (career changers)**
1. The turn: "After N years in X, I moved into Y because..."
2. Transferable proof with a number.
3. Evidence of commitment (course, project, volunteering).
4. What you are looking for.

**C. Early career**
1. Degree/background + the sharpest project or internship result.
2. Skills in action (what you built or analysed).
3. What roles you want and where.

**D. Executive**
1. Mandate and scale (P&L, headcount, geography).
2. Three signature outcomes.
3. Leadership philosophy in one or two sentences.
4. Board/advisory interests, how to engage.

Rules: short paragraphs, no walls of text, no emoji bullets unless the user wants them, no clichés, no unproven superlatives.

## 3. Experience entry pattern
```
Title | Company | Mon YYYY - Present
- Scope: team / budget / users / geography / reporting line
- Result: verb + outcome + number + method
- Result: ...
```
Same facts as the CV; trim to 2-5 lines. Older roles: one line.

## 4. Skills
- Pick the target role's vocabulary (from `job-ad-analyzer` or ads).
- Order by relevance; pin the top 3.
- Include tools and methods, not soft-skill adjectives.
- Prefer the standard LinkedIn skill names so search matches.

## 5. Outreach messages
Connection note (short; free accounts have a lower character limit than Premium; check the editor):
> Hi [Name], I saw your post about [specific thing]. I work on [related field] at [Company] and would value connecting.

Informational request:
> Hi [Name], I'm exploring [role] roles in [industry] and your move from [A] to [B] caught my eye. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat about [one specific question]?

Recruiter message:
> Hi [Name], I noticed you recruit for [team]. I'm a [title] with [one proof point]. I've applied for [role/ref]; happy to share more if useful.

Rules: specific, brief, no copy-paste blasts, no asking for a job in the first message, no false claims of mutual connections.
